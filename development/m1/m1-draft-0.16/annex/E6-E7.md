## 2. E6 DiskLedger (normative)

**N-E6-1 Admission.** Every set (data, checkpoint, tomb) is admitted only if both hold:
- bytes: `inUse + R_bytes + enc + LATE_RESERVE + META_RESERVE ≤ STORE_DISK_HARD`. A tomb skips this byte rule only.
- names: `names + R_names + 1 + LATE_NAMES + EPOCH_NAMES_MAX ≤ 1024`. This applies to tombs as well.

A breach replies `4300 {reason:'disk', limit}` before any set.

**N-E6-2 Reserve.** Check and reserve are one synchronous step. The step adds:
- `enc` to R_bytes (0 for a tomb);
- 1 to R_names;
- one site seat for a creating operation (E7).

It returns a DiskTicket owned by the operation.

**N-E6-3 Remove.** A remove gets a zero ticket. It never enters the live set, and its effect on usage counts only through a measurement.

**N-E6-4 Ticket life.**
1. `live`.
2. At settle the ticket takes `settleSeq = ++seq` and stays counted.
3. It is dropped only in the step that adopts a refresh whose issue sequence number is greater than `settleSeq`.
4. Death drops every ticket. LATE_RESERVE then covers its operations under A15c.

**N-E6-5 Refresh.** Refresh is serialized:
- at most one refresh is in flight;
- a request made while one is in flight is remembered and issued at that completion;
- a refresh is requested after every settle and at every window expiry (E7).

## 3. E6 AdminDelete FSM (D106, D108, D111)

**State.** `DeleteOp {opId, key, state ∈ {running, settled}, attempts[1..2], outcome, requestedAt, resolvedAt, subscribers}`.

**Per-key state.**
- `deleting[key] = opId` while the operation runs.
- `heldOpens[key]` holds the sessions opened while deleting.

**Attempt states:**
- `requested → issued → {failed | expired}`;
- or `requested → slotExpired`;
- or `requested → diskRejected`.

| # | Trigger | Guard | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | deleteSite(key), no op | — | Create op (running), set `deleting[key]`, and run step 2: tear down every frame of the key with «حُذفت بيانات الموقع» (`autoReload: false`). Every queued or ready message becomes suppressed and is released with no reply. Active writes keep their slot and ticket until settle; their replies are suppressed. |
| T2 | deleteSite(key), op running | — | Join: `subscribers += 1`. One promise, no second tomb (x7). |
| T3 | session open while deleting | — | Held; served after resolution (x8). |
| T4 | gate resolves (ready/failed/unrecovered), or no gate | op has no attempt | Attempt 1: AdminFIFO request; its wait timer is 5000 ms. |
| T5 | slot grant | **D111 guard:** request not withdrawn ∧ op running ∧ `deleting[key] = opId` | In ONE step: grant, `DiskLedger.reserve('tomb')`, `SeqAlloc.alloc`, issue the set, start a 5000 ms deadline. If the guard fails: `adminStaleDropped += 1` and the slot passes at once to the next request, with no reserve, no alloc and no set. |
| T6 | tomb settle ok | op running | `confirmed = tomb`, dict `{}`, then the sweep. Resolve **deleted**. |
| T7 | tomb settle ok | op settled | No change (late success; x11). |
| T8 | tomb settle fail / deadline | attempt 1 | Attempt 2 is requested. Attempt 1 keeps its slot until its own settle. |
| T9 | attempt 2 fails or expires | — | Resolve **uncertain** if either attempt expired, else **failed**. |
| T10 | wait expires | attempt 1 | Resolve **adminBusy**. No tomb; the data stays. |
| T11 | wait expires | attempt 2 | **uncertain** if attempt 1 is still pending, else **failed**. |
| T12 | settle (any) | — | The AdminSlot is released by settle **only**: not by a deadline, not by resolution, not by a reply. |

**Resolution** happens in one synchronous step:
1. `op.state = settled`;
2. every queued AdminFIFO request of the op is withdrawn (no seq, no ticket);
3. `deleting[key]` is cleared;
4. held sessions are released.

For a non-success outcome the key becomes `unrecovered`. The next session recovers anew with a checkpoint.

**Bound.** Requested → resolved ≤ 25000 + 2·(5000+5000) = 45000 ms. Every resolved op in every case is checked against it.

**Lemmas (browser.md:737–738, 768–771):**
- **L-E6-1 (deletion):** every set of the key in the generation was allocated before the tomb or after the resolution. So no older snapshot rises above the tomb (x1, x2, x3).
- **L-E6-2 (no late tomb, D111):** a tomb is issued only in a step that passes the guard. Resolution leaves `running` for good, so a resolved op never issues a tomb (x12; the control adminStaleIssue breaks it).
- **L-E6-3 (AdminSlots):** unsettled tombs per generation ≤ 2 at every instant, including death. So each dead generation leaves ≤ 2 late tombs (x9; the control adminEarlyRelease breaks it).

## 4. E7 sites (D112, D113, D114)

- **sitesLive** = keys with ≥ 1 record name in the last adopted measurement, plus any key that is pinned and measured absent (I68 direct predicate).
  - **Pins:** every live non-remove ticket on a key that was counted at its admission pins that key.
  - A pin ends exactly when its ticket is retired by a refresh.
- **Creating operation:** a data set, or a checkpoint for a key that is neither counted nor seat-reserved. A tomb or a remove never creates a key.
- **lateGens(now)** = visible epoch items with E ≤ live E (the live generation's item included) whose contribution has not been dropped. **LATE_SITES = 4·lateGens.**
- **Dropping a contribution:** only in the step that adopts a refresh whose **issue** time is ≥ bootMs(X)+T_LATE. Every expiry schedules a refresh.
- **Admission:**
  - A creating operation is admitted iff `sitesLive + R_sites + LATE_SITES + 1 ≤ 64`, and the seat is taken in the same step.
  - Otherwise the reply is `4300 {reason:'sites', limit:64}`.
  - It adds `retryAfterMs` only when `sitesLive + R_sites + 1 ≤ 64`, i.e. late contributions alone block. The value is the time to the earliest expiry after which the condition holds.
  - Operations on a counted or seat-reserved key never reserve a seat.
- **TombReaper.** It scans at every adopted refresh. A window exit acts through the refresh that the exit issues. It removes a key's sole tomb only if:
  - the key has no session, no live ticket and no pin;
  - every older epoch item has been dropped.

  The remove uses a zero ticket. Capacity is freed only by the measurement that no longer sees the key.

**L-E7-1 (sites lemma; conditional on A15c and A15d).** Every key on disk is:
1. in the last adopted measurement or pinned; or
2. holding a live ticket; or
3. created by an unsettled operation of a dead generation D. There are ≤ 4 such keys per D. They land before bootMs(succ D)+T_LATE, and succ D stays in lateGens until a refresh issued after that point.

Admission keeps the sum ≤ 64, so the number of disk keys stays ≤ 64.

The runner asserts **cases 1–3 independently at every event** (audit `siteLemmaStrayKey` / `siteLemmaLateExceeded`). Explicit A15c/A15d-violated cases are kept apart (§7).

## 6. Event invariants (runner audits)

These are checked after every event of every golden run without a fault:

1. **Seats = issued − retired.** A counter is kept independently from issue, refresh-retirement and death. It must equal R_sites.
2. **Sites lemma:**
   - every disk key is in sitesLive, holds a live ticket, or belongs to a dead-generation operation;
   - the dead-only keys are ≤ LATE_SITES.
3. **Conservative bytes and names:**
   - `inUse ≤ measured + R_bytes + LATE_RESERVE`;
   - `names ≤ measured + R_names + LATE_NAMES + EPOCH_NAMES_MAX`.
4. **Window drop only by a refresh issued at or after the expiry.**
5. **Once-only responses:**
   - one decision per operation id;
   - every message is released exactly once, and there is no `duplicateReply`.
6. **Reaper eligibility** is re-derived at each reaper remove.
7. **Admin:**
   - unsettled tombs ≤ 2;
   - each resolution ≤ 45000 ms;
   - no tomb without a ticket;
   - the version strictly increases per key in issue order (one SeqAlloc per issue);
   - no `recordNameReused`.

## 8. Model conventions (assumptions, not approvals)

- **P-E6-1:** Generation E = 2; reads settle at once (BR24 needs no read holds).
- **P-E6-2:**
  - The attempt-1 AdminFIFO wait timer and the tomb deadline are both 3.0-priority timers; the earlier-scheduled one runs first.
  - A granted request's wait timer is inert.
- **P-E6-3:** BR24 x1 uses W2 = ['k1','y']. Validation leaves W2's pair open; 'k1' is what makes the deleteUnordered failure ("k1 appears") observable.
- **P-E6-4:** A record below the confirmed version that is applied late is removed by the sweep. The model runs that sweep at the apply instant (x2, x3, x5a). An older write that settles after the tomb never replaces the dictionary (browser.md:726).
- **P-E6-5:** A codec limit reached on admission maps to `4300 {reason, limit}` (d7: `entries`, 4096).
- **P-E7-1:** Same-millisecond order: apply/drop 0, death 1, settle 2, refresh completion 3, window-expiry refresh issue 3.5, then requests/scans/probes 4 in schedule order.
- **P-E7-2:**
  - A refresh measures the backend at **completion**. This includes effects that land between issue and completion, which is conservative.
  - Refresh completions are explicit schedule events unless `refresh_delay` is set (d13: 20 ms).
- **P-E7-3:**
  - The epoch item of the live generation appears at boot (confirmation is E2's job), and the boot measurement is adopted.
  - Items of dropped epochs stay visible but contribute nothing.
- **P-E7-4:** `refreshNow` is the test hook of d15c (validation.md:646). The literal "60310" refresh after C0's settle is the refresh that settle issues at 60300 in the model.
- **P-E7-5:** sitesLive at construction equals the measured keys (the "last refresh completed before 0" of d1/d9/d12).

## 9. Findings

- **RF-E6-1 (x12b; baseline conflict, owner/root):**
  - The literal says `adminStaleDropped = 1`.
  - The timeline omits T2a's attempt-1 deadline at 5001 (issued at 1).
  - By D106/D108 that deadline queues T2b. Under adminNoCancel, T2b is not withdrawn when op2 resolves at 7000, and the guard drops it as well.
  - Model: op1 count 1 (the literal intent) and total 2.
  - The runner checks the op1 count and records the total as an explicit partial gap. **No baseline change is proposed.**
- **RF-E7-1 (d1; reaper timing):**
  - D113 scans at every adopted refresh, so the TombReaper may already issue the remove at 250. The literal says 300.
  - The golden reproduces the literal (explicit scan at 300). A variant with the automatic scan shows a remove at 250 and the same states at 400 and 450.
- **RF-E7-2 (d13 premise):** the live generation tries a new site every 1000 ms and fills capacity up to its admission limit. A later generation, which sees more late contributions, can therefore have its two creating operations rejected. So "each dead generation leaves two pending creating operations" need not hold in every placement. By hand, E+2's operations (at 20110/20120, LATE_SITES 8) are expected to be rejected after E+1's tries. The witness histogram records the actual counts; the bound is checked either way.
